# ShareX-Mod v0.6.5-dev — fixed/sticky overlay de-duplication in Recipe capture

Browser-assisted Recipe capture now has a dedicated fixed/sticky overlay policy in addition to the existing pixel-mode static-overlay cleaner.

## Policy

- The first accepted vertical tile may contain the page's fixed/sticky overlay once.
- Later vertical tiles temporarily hide the bounded overlay candidates only while `Page.captureScreenshot` runs.
- The overlay is restored immediately after the tile.
- Semantic click/expand/Next Page actions are performed with overlays restored.
- After a verified page transition, overlay candidates are re-scanned for the new DOM.
- Dynamic-feed captures re-scan candidates after the document grows.
- Horizontal-sweep panels hide page-level fixed/sticky overlays while panel images are being captured.

The page is not permanently restyled. Original inline visibility and priority are preserved and restored.

This specifically targets repeated controls such as floating Back/page indicators without making the matcher depend on those controls for scroll position.

## Separation from normal ShareX capture

Normal pixel scrolling capture continues to use its own `ShareXModStaticOverlayCleaner`. The browser policy only applies to Chrome Enhanced / Capture Recipe paths, where the DOM position model is available.
